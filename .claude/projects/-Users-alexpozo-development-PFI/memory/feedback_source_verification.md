---
name: Source Verification Hard Lesson
description: Industry professional corrected us on incorrect permitting assumptions - zero tolerance for unverified claims about jurisdictions, permits, or regulatory requirements
type: feedback
---

Every claim about a company's permitting pain MUST be jurisdictionally verified before use in outreach or proof sheets. No assumptions, no inferred logic, no "probable scenarios."

**Why:** An industry professional (Constantyn) responded to outreach correcting multiple wrong assumptions: Pecos County TX has no building permits, TCEQ water use authorizations don't apply to that project type, and they weren't tying into FERC-regulated pipelines. The outreach was based on unverified regulatory assumptions that made us look uninformed.

**How to apply:**
1. Before claiming a permit/bottleneck applies, verify the exact jurisdiction requires it for that project type
2. Before claiming a regulatory dependency, confirm the project is actually subject to that agency/regulation
3. Every field in proof sheets and outreach must have a specific source URL proving the exact claim
4. If a permit, constraint, or dependency does not apply in that geography or project structure, the claim is invalid — remove it immediately
5. "No verified source → no claim. No jurisdictional confirmation → no insight. No evidence of active relevance → do not include."
