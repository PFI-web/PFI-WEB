Working doc for the auto repair / inspection shop ledger we've been organizing in Google Sheets.

## ⚠️ GROUND RULE — NEVER CHANGE THE OWNER'S INPUT DATA
**Never add, edit, or overwrite the foundation input data the user typed into the sheet** —
Amount Paid, Part Cost, Product, Date, Type, etc. Do **not** invent or insert transaction
values, and do **not** "normalize" the numbers they entered (no sign flips, rounding, or
reformatting of their figures).

You **may** do things *with* those numbers: add derived/computed columns (e.g. the Labor
formula), totals, summaries, formatting, sorting, new helper tabs — anything that *reads* their
data without altering it. Derived cells must be **formulas that reference** their inputs so the
source numbers stay untouched and authoritative. If a value looks wrong, **flag it** for the
user (e.g. highlight + note) — never silently fix it.

## Column schema
- **Transactions (older):** `Product · Payment Method · Amount Paid · Part Cost · Date · Type · Notes`
- **Transactions (Jun 13+):** `Product · Payment Method · Amount Paid · Part Cost · Profit · Date · Type · Tip · Notes · Inv Item`
  (cols A–J). **The Labor column was removed 2026-06-29** (see below).
- **Profit (per row) = Amount Paid − Part Cost.** Summary reads tabs **by header name**, so
  columns can be reordered/renamed-in-place without breaking anything (but if a TAB is
  renamed, update the two ranges in `summary-tab.mjs`).
- **The three money columns (Jun 13+ tab), in reading order:**
  - **Amount Paid (C)** — owner input: what the customer paid total (this already includes the labor).
  - **Part Cost (D)** — owner input: what the part cost *us*.
  - **Profit (E)** — derived bottom line `=IF(C="","",C-N(D))` (Amount Paid − Part Cost). The
    Amount Paid already bakes in the labor, so subtracting the part cost leaves the profit (= the
    labor/your take). No separate Labor column is needed.
- **Why no Labor column:** parts pass through at cost, so "labor" and "profit" were always the
  same number — a Labor column duplicated Profit. It would only be worth re-adding if parts were
  ever charged *above* cost (a markup); then add a "Part Price" (charged) column with
  Profit = Amount − Part Cost and Labor = Amount − Part Price.
- **Apps Script columns (current):** `C Amount Paid · D Part Cost · E Profit · F Date · G Type ·
  H Tip · I Notes · J Inv Item`. `we-the-best-onedit.gs` references Part Cost col 4 (D), Date col 6
  (F), Type col 7 (G), Profit col 5 (E), Inv Item trigger col 10 (J). The owner must **re-paste**
  it into Extensions → Apps Script after each change.

## Business rules we've established
- **Payments normalized:** every spelling of `efectivo` → **Cash**; `Credit` → **Card reader**;
  `selle`/`zelle` → **Zelle**; `Cash app` → **Cash App**; `n/a` → **N/A**. Combos kept (e.g. "Cash + Card reader").
- **Type = who did the work:** **Mechanic** / **Inspector** / **Expense** (`mecanico` → Mechanic).
- **Oil changes (the tricky one):**
  - `$30` = **labor only**, customer brought own oil → no oil cost.
  - `$80` = customer did **not** bring oil → `$30` of it is our **oil cost** (Part Cost $30, profit $50).
  - Oil-change combos (e.g. "Brake & Oil change") → also $30 oil cost.
  - Other amounts ($35, $65) → can't tell from price → marked **"Verify"**.
  - The **Labor** column on the Jun 13-19 tab tags each oil change: `Labor` / `Oil cost` / `Verify`.
- **Inspection stickers (monthly cost, deducted from inspection profit):**
  - **$1,205/month** before June 2026; **$1,810/month** from June 2026 on (300-sticker batch).
  - Charged only to months that had inspections. Shown in Summary as the Inspector "Cost".
  - Charge legend (context only): `65/70` = discounted both stickers, `50` = safety only, `35/40` = emissions.
- **Tips:** pass-through to mechanics → tracked in the **Tip** column, **excluded from profit**.
- **Expenses:** negative-amount rows (Berger Machine, car wash, etc.) → booked as **Part Cost**,
  Type **Expense** (reduce profit).
- **Daily totals (Jun 13+ tab):** the day's **PURE PROFIT** (`Σ Amount Paid − Σ Part Cost`,
  which subtracts oil costs AND expenses) is written into the gap row under each day, in the
  **Profit** column (E), bold/yellow, labeled "Total made (profit) <date>". These rows have no Type,
  so the Summary ignores them. Note: daily profit does NOT include the monthly inspection
  stickers (those are a monthly cost shown only in the Summary).
- **Misc translations:** `oxygen` means **oil** (oil sensors); `selle`→Zelle, `ganancia`→profit,
  `cliente pago`→client paid, `montura`→mounting, `tuberia`→piping, `gusanillo`→valve stem.
